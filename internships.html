Data Science Intern

The Dow Chemical Company

An AI-enabled product selector that puts product, training and technical knowledge in one place for customer-facing sales representatives and new team members, built and deployed end to end for internal use.

  • Users: customer-facing sales representatives who need product details in front of a customer, and new employees who use it for training and technical onboarding.
  • Model: a selector that works across multiple data modalities, including PowerPoint decks and video, so an answer can come from wherever the knowledge actually lives.
  • Data pipeline: read source data out of SharePoint and built an automated ingestion pipeline, so the data refreshes on its own and the selector stays current without manual updates.
  • Deployment: carried the project from raw source data to a deployed application, used internally by sales representatives and new hires.

Data Science Intern

Hexagon Manufacturing Intelligence

A graph-RAG question-answering system over 20 years of software manuals, built for Applications Engineers.

  • Users: Applications Engineers, who need reliable answers from two decades of technical documentation.
  • Approach: used LangChain to convert document knowledge into a Neo4j graph database, with open-source and Azure models, so retrieval returns context-rich results.
  • Hard cases: supports temporal, vague and reasoning questions over extensive technical documentation.
  • Impact: gives engineers scalable search across the full manual archive.
